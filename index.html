Falali - One AMR Fits All

30.007 Engineering Design Innovation / 2026
Role: Electronics, Firmware, PCB Design
Boards: ESP32-S3, KiCad → JLCPCB
  • A low-profile autonomous mobile robot that moves existing fabric trolleys in a textile factory, with no changes to the trolleys or the factory floor
  • Fits the factory's ≤200 mm trolley clearance and carried the stated ≥100 kg load — demoed with two people on top
  • Owned the electronics and arm firmware end-to-end: 2-layer arm-subsystem and wheel-drive PCBs in KiCad, fabricated through JLCPCB, plus wiring and crimping
  • Wrote the ESP32-S3 arm firmware — non-blocking state machine, time-of-flight sensing, limit-switch homing, servo and BTS7960 motor control, and safety interlocks
  • Base and arm controllers are power-bank powered and talk over WiFi
