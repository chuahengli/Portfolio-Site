Autonomous Material-Flow Robot

30.007 EDI · 2026
Embedded control · PCB design · electromechanical integration

A low-profile autonomous mobile robot designed to move non-standard cage trolleys in a textile manufacturing environment. The system docks under the trolley, extends its arms, clamps the load and releases it safely.

  • My subsystem: designed the arm-subsystem PCB from schematic and netlist through layout and fabrication outputs.
  • Built ESP32-S3 / PlatformIO firmware in C++ around Axis, Arm, Flipper and Motor modules: ToF sensing, servo motion, BTS7960 motor control, hard-stops and a clamp/release state machine.
  • Worked with a PCA9685 PWM driver, four VL53L0X ToF sensors, limit switches and independent X/Y arm axes; verified the pinout against the KiCad netlist.
  • Designed against measurable requirements including ≤200 mm height, ≥300 N clamping force, ≥100 kg load and ≤1 minute docking.

Alleviate — Sit-to-Stand Bed Assist

3D Project · 2026
Engineering analysis · mechatronics · assistive design

A modular add-on to help elderly users move from a bed to standing independently, developed in a five-person ST Engineering For Good team.

  • Owned the CaDDE analysis for the motor subsystem: modelled shaft rotation, carriage displacement and required torque.
  • Ran a 1,000,000-run Monte Carlo simulation across user mass, friction and shaft-distribution assumptions.
  • Estimated ≥1.026 Nm to assist 95% of the target demographic; applied a safety factor of 2 to specify 2.052 Nm across dual 12 V motors.
  • Integrated the analysis with Arduino control, motor drivers, limit switches, an emergency stop and 3D-printed rails.

Parametric Computational Design

Freshmore CTD · 2024
Python-generated geometry · Grasshopper · design for fabrication

A 3D-printed surface generated from mathematical functions, using a Grasshopper circuit and a Python script to explore how parameter changes shape the result.

  • Created a repeating spiral-like structure by varying sine-wave periods, amplitudes and coordinate transforms.

Experimental Modelling: Parachute Drop

Modelling · 2024
Experimental data · regression · calculus optimisation

A team data-analysis project connecting physical measurements to a mathematical model of parachute terminal velocity and drag.

  • Collected accelerometer streams from parachute drops at multiple heights and orientations.
  • Used Excel-based analysis and linear regression to relate parachute area and mass, with a reported R² of 0.995 in the project data.
  • Used calculus and an error-function objective to estimate the drag coefficient and evaluate the model against the experimental observations.

Computational & Data-Driven Engineering

CaDDE · individual · 2026
Machine learning for engineering · modelling · validation

An individual, competition-based course project applying ML/DL to three engineering problems: electrochemical stress regression, engine-pump anomaly detection and battery SEM image classification.

  • Compared a linear Ridge baseline with non-linear XGBoost for electrochemical stress, using feature correlation, engineering intuition and cross-validation to guide model choices.
  • Reasoned about false-negative risk, noisy sensors and drift for engine-pump anomaly detection, including stress-testing ideas for an engineering deployment.
  • Tuned a transfer-learning image classifier for healthy/moderate/severe battery SEM textures; differential learning rates improved reported 5-fold CV accuracy from ~0.72 to ~0.88.
  • Connected model performance to physics and failure modes rather than treating ML as a black box; the work explicitly discusses grey-box and physics-informed modelling.
