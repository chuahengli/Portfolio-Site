Falali - One AMR Fits All

30.007 Engineering Design Innovation / 2026
Role: Electronics, Firmware, PCB Design
Boards: ESP32-S3, KiCad → JLCPCB

Seven-person engineering innovation project building a low-profile autonomous mobile robot (AMR) that moves existing fabric trolleys in a textile factory. The robot drives underneath the trolley, clamps it with extending side arms, and transports it to its destination with no changes to the trolleys or the factory floor. It fits the factory's ≤200 mm trolley clearance, and the stated requirement is moving a ≥100 kg load: the demo unit carried it with two people on top.

I owned the electronics and arm firmware end-to-end. I designed the arm-subsystem and wheel-drive PCBs in KiCad and had them fabricated through JLCPCB, wired and crimped the electronics, and wrote the ESP32-S3 firmware for the arm subsystem, which included the non-blocking state machine, time-of-flight sensing, limit-switch homing, servo and BTS7960 motor control, and the safety interlocks. The robot's two ESP32-S3 controllers (base and arm) are power-bank powered and talk over WiFi.

Alleviate - Sit-to-Stand Bed Assist

Engineering for Good / 2026
Role: computational analysisMethod: 1M-run Monte CarloOutput: motor sizing

Five-person project developing a modular bed attachment that helps elderly users move from sitting to standing: a button press drives synchronised motors to lower the legs to the ground, hold the user as the frame rises, then roll the mat forward. I led the computational analysis — shaft-to-carriage kinematics and a one-million-run Monte Carlo model — which sized the motors at 11.2–13.3 RPM for a 50 cm transfer in about a minute, with torque above 1.02 N·m at a 2× safety factor.

Origaminah - Bird Deterrence

Design Thinking & Innovation / 2025
Role: electrical subsystemPlatform: Arduino NanoOutput: deployable prototype

Five-person team project developing Origaminah, a bird-deterrence device for a semi-outdoor SUTD canteen. When a bird passes, the PIR motion sensor triggers a 3D-printed actuation mechanism that opens the structure to scare it off; the printed modules are wrapped in reflective material, which birds avoid. I developed the electrical subsystem — Arduino Nano, motion sensor, battery, flasher input and wire trunking — routed to the deployable modules, and the team's prototype dismantling drive earned the DTI Sustainability Practice Prize.

Parachute Drop Modelling

Modelling / 2024
Scope: team analysisModel: terminal velocityMethod: regression

Team project on 10.013 Modelling & Analysis modelling an airdropped cargo: a parachute drop from about 8 m, using circular parachutes of radius r with a central rectangular air vent and measured acceleration data. Linear regression on r² yielded mr = 73.334 r² + 10.777, linking total mass and drag coefficient; my contribution covered parts 1(a)–2(c) of the SC08 (Group 9) submission, with a Rhino/Grasshopper parachute model from the DTP component.
