Falali — One AMR Fits All

30.007 EDI · 2026
Autonomous mobile robot · arm electronics · embedded control

A team project developing an autonomous mobile robot for textile-manufacturing trolley transport. Falali is designed to drive underneath a trolley, deploy side-gripping arms and secure the load without modifying the trolley; the portfolio focuses on my arm-subsystem work and the available prototype validation rather than claiming a finished production system.

  • My contribution: designed the arm-subsystem PCB in KiCad, from schematic and interfaces through layout and fabrication outputs.
  • Prototyped and tested the ESP32-S3, VL53L0X ToF sensors, servo flippers, limit switches and external BTS7960 motor interfaces.
  • Developed PlatformIO/Arduino C++ firmware with XSHUT sequencing for four ToF sensors, PCA9685 servo control, homing, hard-stop protection, timeouts, emergency stop and serial diagnostics.
  • Implemented the arm state machine: Homing → Extending → Pre-flip → Flipping → Retracting → Stopped, with a clamp/release sequence.
  • The PDR specified a ≤200 mm height target, ≥300 N holding-force target and ≥100 kg trolley-plus-load target; the reported prototype assessment lists 167 mm AMR height, while the workspace inventory records approximately 70 kg demonstrated in the available media/evidence.

Alleviate — Sit-to-Stand Bed Assist

Engineering for Good · 2026
CaDDE analysis · kinematics · motor sizing

A five-person ST Engineering for Good project developing a modular bed add-on to help elderly users move from lying to standing. The available project files document the Computational and Data-Driven Engineering analysis for the motor subsystem and identify Heng Li as the presenter for the relevant milestone portion; the site does not assign an unsupported sole-owner claim.

  • Modelled the relationship between shaft rotation and carriage displacement for the assist mechanism.
  • Ran a 1,000,000-simulation Monte Carlo model over user mass and friction assumptions using normal and log-normal distributions.
  • The project analysis reports that 95% of users require less than approximately 1.02 Nm per motor, with a 2× safety factor giving a 2.04 Nm total dual-motor operating requirement.
  • Connected the analysis to the system design: Arduino control, motor drivers, limit switches, emergency stop and 3D-printed rails.

Origaminah — Bird Deterrence System

Design Thinking & Innovation · 2025
Product design · Arduino · motion sensing · sustainable prototyping

A five-person team project for bird interference in a semi-outdoor SUTD canteen. Origaminah combines a deployable origami/reflective mechanism with motion-triggered electronics to deter birds while considering user comfort, safety and sustainability.

  • The portfolio evidence covers the electrical and subsystem design: Arduino Nano, PIR motion sensing, battery, wiring trunking and the interface to the actuation mechanism.
  • The team’s DTP documentation describes two PIR sensors detecting motion and triggering the Arduino to command servo-driven movement.
  • Design exploration used the Real-Win-Worth framework and considered UV light, reflective movement, water spray and an opening flower-origami concept before converging on the final system direction.
  • The team received the Sustainability Practice Prize — Prototype Dismantling Drive for the project.

Flexible 2.45 GHz Antenna

Electromagnetics & Apps · 2026
Electrical engineering · CST simulation · parametric automation

A flexible antenna study for the 2.45 GHz ISM band. The work covered a concentric-circle antenna and an EGaIn liquid-metal dipole on kinesiology tape for strain-tolerant wireless applications.

  • Simulated S11, VSWR, radiation and total efficiency, farfield directivity and gain in CST Studio Suite.
  • Created parametric CST VBA macros to speed up geometry iteration and compare design variants.

Engineering Machine Learning

CaDDE · individual project · 2026
Machine learning for engineering · modelling · validation

An individual project applying machine learning and deep learning to engineering problems: electrochemical-stress regression, engine-pump anomaly detection and battery SEM image classification.

  • Compared a Ridge baseline with XGBoost for electrochemical stress, using feature relationships and cross-validation to guide model selection.

Parachute Drop Modelling

Modelling · 2024
Experimental data · regression · calculus optimisation

A team data-analysis project connecting physical measurements to a mathematical model of parachute terminal velocity and drag.

  • Collected accelerometer streams from parachute drops at multiple heights and orientations.
  • Used Excel-based analysis and linear regression to relate parachute area and mass; the project data reports an R² of 0.995.
  • Used calculus and an error-function objective to estimate the drag coefficient and compare the model against observations.
