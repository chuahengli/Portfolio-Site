Motor specification derivation

Worst case: m = 100 kg, μ = 0.20
F_friction = 0.20 × 100 × 9.81 = 196 N
r_shaft = 0.0125 m
T_required = 196 × 0.0125 = 2.45 Nm
Safety factor ×2 → T_design = 4.90 Nm

Selected motor torque

≥ 5.0 Nm

Covers the majority of users:

Singapore elderly avg weight ~55–75 kg
μ for nursing slide sheet ≈ 0.05–0.12
Motor at 5 Nm handles up to 100 kg at μ = 0.25 before safety factor is breached

Design insight: reducing μ through better mat material has a larger proportional impact on required torque than reducing user weight — mat selection is the highest-leverage design variable.
